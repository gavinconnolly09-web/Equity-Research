# Intel (INTC) Equity Research Report

## Overview

This project evaluates Intel Corporation (NASDAQ: INTC) through a comprehensive equity research framework, including financial analysis, segment analysis, peer comparison, valuation, and investment recommendation.

The objective of this report was to determine whether Intel's recent turnaround narrative is supported by underlying business fundamentals and whether the company's current valuation appropriately reflects the risks and opportunities associated with its Foundry transformation strategy.

## Key Areas Analyzed

* Company Overview & Initial Investment Thesis
* Financial Analysis
* Segment Analysis
* Peer Comparison (Nvidia, AMD, and TSMC)
* Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation
* Key Risks & Catalysts
* Investment Thesis & Recommendation

## Key Findings

* Revenue appears to have stabilized following the sharp declines experienced between 2022 and 2024.
* Intel's core businesses remain profitable, particularly Client Computing Group (CCG) and Data Center & AI (DCAI).
* Intel Foundry remains the primary source of profitability pressure, although recent results suggest losses may be beginning to moderate.
* Peer analysis shows Intel operates with significantly lower margins than major semiconductor competitors.
* DCF analysis indicates that current market expectations already reflect a meaningful portion of Intel's expected turnaround.

## Investment Recommendation

**Hold / Neutral**

While Intel has demonstrated several encouraging signs, including revenue stabilization, improving DCAI performance, and moderating Foundry losses, significant uncertainty remains surrounding long-term Foundry profitability and the returns generated by the company's manufacturing investments.
